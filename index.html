Questions people ask
Do I need an account?

No. You can scan a leaf and get the damage stage and confidence straight away. An account (free) adds your scan history, the spread risk near your farm, advisor help and outbreak alerts.

How accurate is it?

AI results can be wrong. Before spraying, talk to an advisor and follow the product label.

What happens to my photo?

Photos you scan and questions you type in the chat are sent to Google's Gemini service. On the free plan, Google may use them to improve its products, and people at Google may read them. Do not upload photos that show people or type personal details.

Which crops and languages?

Maize, beans, potato, rice, cassava, banana, sweet potato, sorghum, groundnut, soybean, coffee, tea and vegetables — in English and Kinyarwanda.

About and privacy

If you create an account, your scans, farm details and help requests are stored in Umurima's database so you and the advisors helping you can see them. Advisors see reports from their area; administrators manage the system.
