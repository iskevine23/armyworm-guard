About and privacy

Photos you scan and questions you type in the chat are sent to Google's Gemini service. On the free plan, Google may use them to improve its products, and people at Google may read them. Do not upload photos that show people or type personal details.

If you create an account, your scans, farm details and help requests are stored in ArmywormGuard's database so you and the advisors helping you can see them. Advisors see reports from their area; administrators manage the system.

AI results can be wrong. Before spraying, talk to an advisor and follow the product label.
